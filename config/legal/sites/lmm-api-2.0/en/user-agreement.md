## Supplement on background content review and account balances

OpenAI Moderation review for the built-in AI assistant and for API safety auditing is off by default. Administrators may enable lenient or strict review for specified account groups. We must publish the scope, mode, category amounts and effective time in advance and follow the notice rules above for material changes. A feature announcement does not activate any group or authorize a deduction for a request made before the rule took effect.

Review runs in the background. Ordinary requests proceed without waiting for classification. The feature does not promise real-time blocking or detection of every violation. It classifies only text within supported limits that was actually extracted and successfully reviewed. For recognized API protocols, it takes the text of the last user message. For the built-in assistant, it takes the current user input and records the assistant’s reply separately. It does not submit the entire request JSON, the full conversation, system prompts, tool definitions or tool results. It skips unrecognized request structures, images, audio, video and binary files. Failed calls, malformed responses, over-limit material and unreviewed content do not count as violations, incur a deduction or increase a risk score.

In lenient mode, a flagged user input triggers an in-service warning only, with no deduction from the balance. In strict mode, a flagged input may trigger a deduction from your account balance based on the published US dollar amount for the category. If one request triggers several categories, only the highest configured amount applies, once. An unset or zero amount produces no deduction. We round down to the smallest ledger unit. If the amount is below one unit, you receive a warning only. The actual deduction cannot exceed the configured amount or your available positive balance at the time of processing. It cannot create debt, trigger a top-up or payment card charge, or be recovered from a later top-up. A retried request cannot cause a duplicate deduction. A later increase in a category amount cannot increase an earlier charge. In-service notices and account records distinguish the rule amount from the actual deduction.

The platform sets strict-mode deductions under its published rules. They do not represent a fee for calling OpenAI’s Moderation API. We record model output separately. Model output is not attributed to you and does not cause a deduction from your balance or increase your violation risk score. Only flagged user inputs that completed review count toward the score, once per request. After the last administrative reset, the review score reaches 0.4 at 1 violating request, 0.6 at 2, 0.8 at 5 and 0.95 at 10. The final account risk score is the higher of the review score and the existing wallet risk score. The reviewed-request count is simply a count. The score is a heuristic for account administration. It does not measure the probability of unlawful conduct or confidence in the classification. A classification flag does not establish that conduct was unlawful.

Classifications can be wrong. You may request human review of a category, amount or risk record through in-service support or [support@lmm.best](mailto:support@lmm.best). Existing dispute procedures and statutory rights continue to apply. See the [safety review description](/legal/safety-review.html) and the [published safety rules](/security).
