### 7.1 General rule

We deliver digital services immediately after payment. **A top-up generally does not qualify for a refund once any part of that top-up has been used.** Gift, promotional, test, rebate and compensation credits cannot be redeemed for cash or refunded. Mandatory rights under applicable law take precedence.

### 7.2 When you can request a refund

1. **Entirely unused top-up within 7 days.** You can request a full refund within 7 calendar days of a successful top-up if none of the balance from that transaction has been used, transferred or redeemed.
2. **Duplicate charge.** If a system or payment processing error charged you more than once for the same order, we will refund the verified duplicate amount.
3. **Confirmed billing error.** If a platform settlement error resulted in an excessive or incorrect charge, we will refund the amount charged in error after verification.
4. **Payment completed but service not delivered.** You can request a refund if the platform failed to credit a completed payment and we cannot provide the corresponding balance or entitlement within a reasonable time.
5. **Service discontinued by us.** If we discontinue the service for reasons unrelated to a breach by you, we may refund the unused paid balance refundable by law or agree with you on equivalent compensation.
6. **Statutory rights.** You may exercise any right of withdrawal, refund or other consumer right that applies to you and cannot be waived.

### 7.3 When a refund is unavailable

Except as provided in section 7.2 or under applicable law, refunds are unavailable for the following:

1. A top-up that has been used in whole or in part.
2. An unused balance for which you request a refund for personal reasons after 7 calendar days.
3. Ordinary usage resulting from overlooking the pricing page, choosing the wrong model, a configuration error, lost credentials or someone else using your account.
4. Gift, promotional, test, rebate, redemption or compensation credits.
5. An account suspension or termination resulting from fraud, unlawful activity, abuse or another serious breach of the Terms of Service.
6. A third-party model service delivered normally and billed correctly whose output did not meet your expectations.

### 7.4 How to request a refund

Email [support@lmm.best](mailto:support@lmm.best) from your registered email address. Use “Refund request” as the subject. Include:

- Your registered email address.
- The order number or transaction ID.
- The date and time of the top-up and the amount.
- The reason for your request and any necessary supporting evidence.

We usually acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and complete our review or give you a progress update within 5 business days. We generally submit an approved refund to the original payment channel within 5 business days after completing the review. Funds usually arrive in 5–10 business days, depending on the payment processor, card issuer or bank.

When the refund is complete, we remove the corresponding balance or entitlement from your account. While reviewing your request, we may temporarily freeze the unused balance corresponding to the amount requested to prevent it from being settled twice.
